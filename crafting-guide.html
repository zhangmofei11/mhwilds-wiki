CRAFTING GUIDE

Master equipment progression

⚔️ WEAPON PROGRESSION

Early Game (HR 1-4)

Craft Jagras or Izuchi weapons. Focus on one weapon type.

Mid Game (HR 5-7)

Royal Ludroth for water element. Arzuros for raw damage.

Late Game (HR 8+)

Start crafting Elder Dragon weapons. Focus on meta builds.

🛡️ ARMOR PROGRESSION

Defense Priority

  • • Upgrade armor regularly
  • • Match defense to monster damage
  • • Get 50+ defense before Arzuros
  • • Get 100+ before Rathalos

Skill Focus

  • • Early game: Defense skills
  • • Mid game: Attack + Crit
  • • Late game: Meta skills

🌾 MATERIAL FARMING

Low Rank

  • • Hunt target monsters
  • • Break parts for bonus drops
  • • Capture for rare materials

High Rank

  • • Hunt same monsters HR version
  • • Use Investigation quests
  • • Collect bone piles efficiently

End Game

  • • Event quests for rare items
  • • Arena quests for guaranteed parts
  • • Target specific investigations

💎 RARE ITEM CRAFTING

Tickets & Vouchers

  • • Palamute Silversmith Ticket
  • • Hunter's VIP Ticket
  • • Account Upgrade Materials

Limited Items

  • • Feathercone Shrooms
  • • Dragonvein Coal
  • • Elder Dragon Blood

💡 CRAFTING TIPS

Efficiency

  • • Always carry crafting materials
  • • Set loadouts for different hunts
  • • Bulk craft potions before expeditions

Priorities

  • 1. Weapon upgrade first
  • 2. Armor defense next
  • 3. Then optimize skills
