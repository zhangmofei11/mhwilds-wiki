HUNTING GUIDE

Master the hunt with proven strategies

📋 PRE-HUNT PREPARATION

Essential Items

  • • Health Potions x10
  • • Max Potions x3
  • • Antidotes x5
  • • Whetfish x20
  • • Barrel Bombs x5

Weapon Check

  • • Weapon sharpness at green or higher
  • • Coatings ammo fully stocked
  • • Armor fully repaired
  • • Equipment optimized for target

⚔️ COMBAT TIPS

Study the Monster

Watch attack patterns before engaging. Learn safe windows.

Aim for Weak Spots

Head for stun, wings for cut damage, legs for trips.

Manage Stamina

Don't spam attacks. Conserve stamina for dodge rolls.

🐉 MONSTER MECHANICS

Status Effects

  • • POISON - Damage over time
  • • PARALYSIS - Monster stunned
  • • STUN - Head hits cause stun
  • • ICE - Slows monster

Part Breaking

  • • Break parts for bonus materials
  • • Broken parts reduce attacks
  • • Mount damage to topple monster
  • • Sever tails for carve chance

🎯 HUNT STRATEGIES

Early Game - Learn Patterns

Focus on dodging, not dealing damage. Study every attack.

Mid Game - Optimize Damage

Chain attacks into combos. Use terrain for advantage.

Late Game - Speed Runs

Min-max builds. Skip optional steps. Maximize DPS windows.

👥 MULTIPLAYER TIPS

Coordination

  • • Focus fire on same part
  • • Communicate monster status
  • • Assign roles (tank, DPS, support)

Aggro Management

  • • Draw aggro with low damage
  • • Tank distracts from DPS
  • • Support heals at right times
